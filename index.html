How it works

Simple help, built for real life.

Choose the moment you are dealing with, build something useful, then take it into your family routine.

01 · Choose

Start with one real problem.

Pick the tool that matches what is happening right now. No account, setup or long questionnaire required.

02 · Build

Get something made for your situation.

Answer a few useful questions and receive a practical plan, script, activity or family agreement.

03 · Use

Take it off the screen.

Print it, save it, copy it or simply use it tonight. The goal is a useful next step — not more information.

About Family Screen Guide

Family Screen Guide exists to make everyday screen-time moments easier to handle.

Instead of telling families that there is one perfect way to manage technology, we focus on practical tools for real moments: transitions, conversations, family boundaries, routines and offline alternatives.

The aim is simple: come with a problem and leave with something you can use.
